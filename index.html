Welcome to Bananas Rogai. Where quality is our mission.

Bananas Rogai was founded through the entrepreneurship and vision of Rodolfo Gaibor. Together with his family, he has sustained green plantain fields for more than 50 years, producing and selling locally.

Our plantains grow 300 to 400 meters above sea level on Ecuador’s Pacific Coast. We use that clean environment to make snacks free of chemicals and respectful of the land, because we believe that is the key to success.

Our organic farming supports the healthy, delicious snacks you find in every Rogai bag.
